PIGEON

" Pigeons belonging to the Columbidae family (order Columbiformes). Doves are typically used for smaller forms, and pigeons for larger ones. The white domestic pigeon, also known as the “dove of peace,” is an exception. For thousands of years, pigeons have played important roles as food, pets, sacred animals, mail couriers, and more. Pigeons are first mentioned in writing 5,000 years ago in Mesopotamia. For ritual purposes, the ancient Egyptians would offer tens of thousands of victims at once. "
