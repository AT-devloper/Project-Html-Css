PARROT

" The most well-liked and well-known bird is the parrot. In addition to being stunning with its vividly colored feathers and beak, it is regarded as one of the smartest birds. However, a parrot is more than just a colorfully plumed bird with exceptional imitation abilities. The majority of parrot's meals mostly consist of seeds, nuts, fruit, buds, and other plant material. The major habitat of a parrot is warmer climates, Which are typically associated with jungle or rainforest. "
