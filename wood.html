WOODPECKER

" With the exception of Australia, New Guinea, New Zealand, Madagascar, and most polar regions, woodpeckers are members of the family Picidae. The majority of species graze primarily for insect prey on tree trunks and branches. They frequently communicate by drumming with their beaks, which produces a reverberating sound that may be heard from a distance. Fruits, bird eggs, small animals, tree sap, human food scraps, and carrion are some of the different foods that some species eat. Their abandoned holes are significant to other cavity-nesting birds because they often nest and sleep in holes they bore in tree trunks. "
